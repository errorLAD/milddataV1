Frequently Asked Questions
How is fuel efficiency (L/Hr) calculated?
Fuel efficiency is computed by dividing total refuel liters by operating hours logged since previous fill-up. If consumption exceeds baseline thresholds (>15 L/Hr), an abnormal flag is auto-triggered.
How do Document Expiry alerts work?
Documents expiring within 30 days show a yellow warning pill. Documents past their expiration date trigger a red EXPIRED alert pill and dashboard notice.
How does the AI Assistant analyze my fleet?
The AI Assistant queries your organization's live SQL database records in real-time to compute profit, idle machines, maintenance due, and repair-vs-replace ratios.
